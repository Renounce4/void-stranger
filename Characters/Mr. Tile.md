# Combat
Taking a tile he is part of hurts him.
Takes up a 2x4

## Phase 1
Disappears after I take 7 actions at first, but gets faster by 1 after each hit.
Reappears after 2 more - always appearing to spawn in corners (though sometimes in the middle).

## Phase 2
Now he stays in place until I get close enough to hurt him, and he then immediately vanishes.
I instead make the void move to him. One hit is all it takes, killing [[Mr. Tile]], one hit is all it takes.
