Mirrors my actions. Can punch, but cannot use items.
Walking in to him kills me.

Since he doesn't have [[Void Wings]], floating over a void is a way to kill him in a symmetrical layout.

Behaves like normal enemies when bumping in to other enemies.
If he attempts to occupy the same tile as another enemy on the same turn, they both die. Otherwise, he blocks enemies.
